FAQ

Transparency in Every Step

Vesdea is a private market advisory firm. We help startups raise capital through fundraising rounds (primary market) and facilitate the transfer of equity positions between shareholders and investors (secondary market). We manage the entire process from sourcing to closing.

We work alongside you to identify and connect with the right investors for your round — private investors, family offices, and funds. We handle investor sourcing, deal structuring, and support you through the negotiation and closing process.

We connect founders, early employees, and early investors with qualified buyers who want to acquire their equity position. The process is handled privately and discreetly, and we structure the transaction to ensure a clean execution that respects the company's cap table.

We source exclusive opportunities in high-growth startups — both primary rounds and secondary positions. These are deals that are typically not available on the open market. We handle all the due diligence and structuring so you can focus on the investment decision.
